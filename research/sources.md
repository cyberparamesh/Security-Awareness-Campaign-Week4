# Research Sources & Authoritative References

This document catalogs the authoritative cybersecurity standards, government awareness campaigns, and industry training frameworks referenced in the **Week 4 Security Awareness Campaign Planning** project.

All cited sources are public, peer-reviewed, and ethical benchmarks designed to foster human risk management and enterprise security culture.

---

## 1. Primary Standards & Government Frameworks

### 1. CISA "Secure Our World" Public Awareness Campaign
* **Publisher:** Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA), U.S. Department of Homeland Security
* **Publication Date:** October 2023 / Updated 2025/2026
* **Key Topics Covered:** Four Core Actions for Digital Safety:
  1. Use Strong Passwords & Password Managers
  2. Turn On Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  3. Recognize and Report Phishing
  4. Update Software Promptly
* **URL:** [https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world](https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world)
* **Application in Project:** Formed the core behavioral messaging pillars for employee communications and micro-learning modules.

### 2. NIST SP 800-50: Building an Information Technology Security Awareness and Training Program
* **Publisher:** National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), U.S. Department of Commerce
* **Publication Date:** October 2003 (Updated Framework Guidance 2024)
* **Key Topics Covered:** Lifecycle approach to learning programs (Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate), distinction between Awareness vs. Training vs. Education, organizational alignment.
* **URL:** [https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-50/final](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-50/final)
* **Application in Project:** Provided the program lifecycle architecture, content development methodology, and effectiveness measurement framework.

### 3. NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: Awareness and Training (AT Control Family)
* **Publisher:** NIST
* **Publication Date:** September 2020 (Updated 2024)
* **Key Controls Covered:** 
  - `AT-1` Literacy Training and Awareness Policy and Procedures
  - `AT-2` Literacy Training and Security Awareness
  - `AT-3` Role-Based Security Training
  - `AT-4` Training Records Management
* **URL:** [https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53/rev-5/final](https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-53/rev-5/final)
* **Application in Project:** Utilized to structure general workforce awareness, executive briefings, and role-based developer/finance training.

### 4. SANS Security Awareness Maturity Model
* **Publisher:** SANS Institute / SANS Security Awareness
* **Publication Date:** 2023 / 2025 Benchmarks
* **Key Maturity Levels:** Level 1 (Non-existent), Level 2 (Compliance Focused), Level 3 (Promoting Awareness & Behavior Change), Level 4 (Long-Term Culture Change), Level 5 (Metrics Framework).
* **URL:** [https://www.sans.org/security-awareness-training/](https://www.sans.org/security-awareness-training/)
* **Application in Project:** Used to benchmark organizational maturity transition from Level 2 (Compliance) to Level 4 (Culture Change).

### 5. CISA Phishing Guidance & Phishing-Resistant MFA Advisories
* **Publisher:** CISA
* **Publication Date:** 2024 / 2025
* **Key Topics Covered:** Social engineering tactic recognition, reporting mechanisms, Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) resistance, simulated phishing program ethics.
* **URL:** [https://www.cisa.gov/phishing](https://www.cisa.gov/phishing)
* **Application in Project:** Guided the design of Section 8 (Phishing & Social Engineering) and simulated phishing campaign metrics.

## 2. Ethical Compliance Declaration

All campaign activities, gamification ideas, and simulated phishing protocols described in this repository are strictly defensive and designed to empower employees. Simulated phishing tests must follow "no-shame" educational principles and provide immediate point-of-failure micro-learning rather than punitive measures.
